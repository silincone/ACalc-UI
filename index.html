    Add a subject
    S

    Import attendance
    A

    Move between subjects, once one is focused
    → ←
    Open or close the focused subject
    E P Del
    Edit, project or delete the focused subject
    F2
    Rename, while editing a subject
    A S
    Attend or skip, in the projection dialog
